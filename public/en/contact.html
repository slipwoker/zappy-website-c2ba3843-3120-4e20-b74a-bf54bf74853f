Frequently Asked Questions

Here you'll find answers to the most frequently asked questions our customers ask. If you didn't find what you were looking for, we're here for you.

How do you choose the right size for bedding?

The right size depends on your mattress size. Bedding for single bed (90x200 cm), bed and a half (120x200 cm), double bed (160x200 cm) and large double bed (180x200 cm). We recommend measuring the mattress before purchase and ensuring the fitted sheet includes elastic that fits the mattress thickness.

What's the difference between different types of towels?

Regular cotton towels are perfect for daily use, bamboo towels are extra soft with high absorbency, and microfiber towels dry quickly and are suitable for sports and travel. We recommend quality cotton towels for families with children and bamboo towels for a home spa experience.

How do you properly care for bedding and towels?

Wash in lukewarm water (40 degrees) with gentle detergent, without fabric softener which may reduce absorbency. Dry on low-medium temperature or air dry to maintain softness and quality over time. Light ironing on low-medium temperature improves appearance and adds a luxurious feel.

What's included in a bedding and towel gift set?

Our gift sets typically include a complete bedding set (fitted sheet, duvet and pillowcases), towel set (2 bath towels and 2 hand towels), and designer gift packaging. The set can be customized upon request and additional blankets or pillows can be added. It's a perfect gift for young couples, weddings and celebrations.

How do your personal recommendations work?

We listen to your needs – number of family members, preferences for softness or absorbency, design style, and budget. After a brief conversation, we recommend the most suitable products from our range of bedding and towels. Our goal is for you to feel wrapped in love and maximum comfort at home.
