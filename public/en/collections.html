Simple guidelines that will extend the life of your products and maintain the perfect feel you love.

Washing and Drying

Wash in lukewarm water up to 40 degrees with gentle detergent. Avoid bleach or strong fabric softeners – they may damage natural fibers and reduce absorbency.

Air dry or machine dry on low temperature. Excessive drying may cause slight shrinkage and loss of softness. Remove from dryer while products are still slightly damp.

Ironing is not necessary for most of our bedding and towels. If you choose to iron, use medium temperature and avoid direct ironing on embroidery or decorative details.

Storage and Longevity

Store bedding and towels in a dry, ventilated place, away from direct sunlight. Proper folding and storage on open shelves will prevent musty odors and allow fibers to breathe.

Rotate between different sets to reduce wear. Alternating use extends product life and allows fibers to recover between washes.

Check products periodically for small tears or loose threads. Immediate repair will prevent further damage and maintain the original appearance and feel over time.
